Speaker Script / 演讲稿: LED runs in SNOLAB Run 7

约 10 分钟，13 页正片，后面是备用页（只在被问到时用）。每页先中文、后英文，意思一样，英文可以直接照着念。

时间（秒）：1 Title 15，2 Background 45，3 Example LED run 40，4 How a trigger is matched to an LED 40，5 Visibility: phase 1 55，6 Visibility: phase 2 35，7 Stability: amplitude vs time 50，8 Amplitude: cases to look at again 55，9 Stability: trigger fraction vs time 55，10 Template generation: method 65，11 Templates: example 30，12 Example pulses: Z7 PSum with Z7's LED 55，13 Summary 40；合计约 580 秒。

Slide 1: Title（约 15 秒）

中文：大家好，我是李志恒。今天讲 Run 7 中子活化之后的 LED 数据。讲四点：谁看到哪个 LED，幅度稳不稳，触发稳不稳，还有脉冲模板。

English: Hi everyone, I'm Zhiheng. Today I'll show the post-activation LED data from Run 7. Four points: who sees which LED, amplitude stability, trigger stability, and pulse templates.

Slide 2: Background（约 45 秒）

中文：先说背景。这是 Run 7 在中子活化之后的数据。DAQ 脚本一直循环：先取低本底数据，再取一个 LED run，大概每 2 个小时一次。上面每一条竖线是一个 LED run。从 9 月 15 号到 21 号一共 47 个，我们用了 45 个。

English: First, the background. This is Run 7 data after the neutron activation. The DAQ script runs in a loop: low-background data, then one LED run, about every 2 hours. Each line on the top is one LED run. From September 15 to 21 there are 47 of them, and we use 45.

Slide 3: Example LED run（约 40 秒）

中文：这是一个 LED run 的例子。第一段 15 个 LED 在闪，第二段换 12 个 LED。每个 LED 每秒闪一次，一共 151 次。每次触发只读出触发的那个探测器。

English: Here is one LED run as an example. In phase one, 15 LEDs flash. In phase two, 12 LEDs flash. Each LED flashes once per second, 151 times in total. Each trigger reads out only the detector that triggered.

Slide 4: How a trigger is matched to an LED（约 40 秒）

中文：怎么知道一个触发来自哪个 LED？每个 LED 每秒闪一次，而且每个 LED 在一秒里闪的时刻不一样。所以我们画触发时间在这一秒里的位置，每个 LED 就是一个峰。落在峰里的触发，就算这个 LED 的。脚本告诉我们哪些 LED 开着，LED 自己的探测器会在它的峰里触发，这样就知道每个峰是哪个 LED。峰外面零散的触发是本底。两个 LED 相差不到 0.2 毫秒时分不开，这种情况不用，后面的图上是灰色。

English: How do we know which LED a trigger comes from? Each LED flashes once per second, and each LED has its own time within the second. So we plot the trigger time within its second, and each LED is one peak. A trigger in a peak belongs to that LED. The script tells us which LEDs are on, and the LED's own detector triggers in its peak, so we know which LED each peak is. The few triggers outside the peaks are background. If two LEDs flash within 0.2 milliseconds, we cannot separate them. We leave those out, and they are grey in the later plots.

Slide 5: Visibility: phase 1（约 55 秒）

中文：这是第一段的可见度图。横轴是 LED，纵轴是探测器。格子里的数是：这个 LED 闪的时候，这个探测器触发的比例。红框是 LED 自己的探测器，基本都是 1。红框旁边的格子，是隔壁的探测器也看到了光。

English: This is the visibility plot for phase one. Columns are LEDs, rows are detectors. The number is how often the detector triggers when that LED flashes. The red box is the LED's own detector, and it is almost always one. A number next to a red box means the neighbouring detector also sees the light.

Slide 6: Visibility: phase 2（约 35 秒）

中文：第二段也一样。自己的探测器基本每次都触发，隔壁的探测器也经常触发。光只到相邻的探测器，再远就没有了。

English: Phase two shows the same pattern. The own detector triggers almost every time, and often the next detector too. The light only reaches the next detector, never further.

Slide 7: Stability: amplitude vs time（约 50 秒）

中文：第二点，幅度随时间。每一行是一个探测器看一个 LED，每一列是一个 series，按时间排。左边是第一段，右边是第二段。白色就是平均水平。大部分是白的，91% 的点变化在 10% 以内。所以大部分是稳定的，但有几个地方要再看。

English: Second, amplitude versus time. Each row is one detector with one LED, each column is one series, in time order. Phase one is on the left, phase two on the right. White means average. Most of it is white: 91 percent of the points are within 10 percent. So it is mostly stable, but a few spots need a second look.

Slide 8: Amplitude: cases to look at again（约 55 秒）

中文：这几个要再看。最明显的是 9 月 18 号凌晨：Z20 低了很多，Z19 看 Z20 的 LED 也低，过了几个小时恢复了。运行日志里写着：9 月 17 号晚上十点，冷水机报警，探测器被放进了 safe state。所以可能是 Z19 和 Z20 的一些 HEMT 设置变了。每个点上的红色短线是误差棒。另外，第一天有几个格子偏高一点。

English: These need a second look. The clearest one: early on September 18, Z20 is much lower, and Z19 with Z20's LED is lower too. A few hours later they are back. The operation elog says that on September 17 at 10 pm there were chiller alarms, and the detectors were put into safe state. So some HEMT settings of Z19 and Z20 might have changed. The small red bars are the error bars. Also, on the first day a few cells are a bit high.

Slide 9: Stability: trigger fraction vs time（约 55 秒）

中文：第三点，触发比例随时间。黄色是每次闪光都触发。自己 LED 的那些行一直是黄的，很稳定。其他行有台阶。台阶正好在触发阈值被改的地方，就是图上的黑线。比如运行日志里记着，9 月 18 号零点十分，9 号、10 号、11 号、15 号探测器的阈值被调高了。所以这是设置变了，不是探测器不稳定。

English: Third, trigger fraction versus time. Yellow means it triggers on every flash. The own-LED rows stay yellow: stable. Other rows have steps. The steps are exactly where the trigger thresholds were changed, the black lines. For example, the elog says that on September 18 at ten past midnight the thresholds of detectors 9, 10, 11 and 15 were raised. So these are setting changes, not unstable detectors.

Slide 10: Template generation: method（约 65 秒）

中文：第四点是模板。先说一下模板是怎么做的。我们先选事件，就是这个 LED 闪的时候、这个探测器触发的事件，十二个通道都用。然后用一个很短的中值滤波，去掉 LED 通电带来的窄尖峰。接着做 100 千赫的低通滤波，再减掉基线，除以峰值。之后，每个脉冲用两个指数拟合，起始时间是自由的。右上图就是一个脉冲和它的拟合。有了起始时间，就可以把实测的脉冲对齐。最后，把 45 个 series 所有对齐的脉冲取平均，这就是模板，也就是右下图红色的线。所以拟合只是用来找起始时间，模板本身是实测脉冲的平均。另外，我们还用一个上升、两个下降的函数去拟合模板，得到解析脉冲。

English: The fourth point is the templates. Let me first show how we make them. We start with the events where this detector triggered on this LED's flash, and we use all twelve channels. First, a very short median filter removes the narrow spike from the LED drive. Then we apply a 100 kilohertz low-pass filter. Next, we subtract the baseline and divide by the peak. After that, we fit each pulse with two exponentials, and the start time is free. You can see one pulse and its fit at the top right. With the fitted start time, we shift the measured pulses so that they all start at the same time. Finally, we average all the aligned pulses of the 45 series. This average is the template, the red line at the bottom right. So the fit is only used to find the start time, and the template itself is the average of the measured pulses. In the end, we also fit the template with one rise time and two fall times. This gives the analytic pulse.

Slide 11: Templates: example（约 30 秒）

中文：这是 Z2 的例子，十二个通道。蓝色是对齐后的单个脉冲，红色是模板，黑色虚线是解析脉冲。标题是灰色的通道，没有清楚的脉冲，所以不拟合解析脉冲。

English: Here is Z2 as an example, twelve channels. Blue are the aligned single pulses, red is the template, and the dashed black line is the analytic pulse. Channels with a grey title have no clear pulse, so we do not fit an analytic pulse there.

Slide 12: Example pulses: Z7 PSum with Z7's LED（约 55 秒）

中文：我们要看一下脉冲形状随时间怎么变。这是 Z7 看自己的 LED，十二个通道加起来，就是 PSum。每个 series 取一个脉冲，一共 45 个。颜色从浅到深是从早到晚：黄色最早，紫色最晚。每个脉冲先过一个 40 千赫的低通滤波，把高频噪声去掉，再除以自己的峰值，只看形状。这些脉冲基本叠在一起，说明这一周形状没有变。脉冲前面那个很窄的尖峰是 LED 驱动的串扰。

English: We want to see how the pulse shape changes with time. This is Z7 with its own LED, all twelve channels summed: the PSum. One pulse per series, 45 in total. Light to dark is early to late: yellow is the first day, purple the last. Each pulse goes through a 40 kilohertz low-pass filter to remove high-frequency noise, and is divided by its own peak, so we only see the shape. The pulses lie on top of each other: the shape did not change during the week. The narrow spike just before the pulse is pickup from the LED drive.

Slide 13: Summary（约 40 秒）

中文：总结一下。先说可见度，每个 LED 自己的探测器都看得到，隔壁的也常常看到。幅度方面，大部分是稳定的，只有 9 月 18 号 Z19 和 Z20 偏低，那是在探测器进入 safe state 之后。触发比例也是稳定的，台阶是因为阈值改了。最后是模板，每个格子都有一个，Z7 的脉冲形状这一周没有变。谢谢大家。

English: To sum up. For visibility, each LED is seen by its own detector, and often by the next one as well. In terms of amplitude, it is mostly stable. Only Z19 and Z20 were low on September 18, after the detectors were put into safe state. The trigger fraction is also stable, and the steps come from threshold changes. Finally, we have a template for every cell, and the Z7 pulse shape did not change during the week. Thank you.

可能被问的问题 / Likely questions（回答一两句就够）

Q1. 为什么看不到更远的探测器？/ Why no detector further away?

中文：我们只读出触发的探测器。光如果太弱、没过阈值，就看不到。

English: Only the triggering detector is read out. If the light is below threshold, we cannot see it.

Q2. 9 月 18 日 Z19、Z20 为什么偏低？/ Why are Z19 and Z20 low on 18 September?

中文：运行日志里写着，9 月 17 日 22:04 冷水机报警，探测器被放进 safe state。恢复之后，Z19 和 Z20 的一些 HEMT 设置可能变了。到下午一点就正常了。

English: The operation elog says that on 17 September at 22:04 there were chiller alarms and the detectors were put into safe state. After the recovery, some HEMT settings of Z19 and Z20 might have changed. By 1 pm it was back to normal.

Q3. 触发阈值是谁改的？/ Who changed the trigger thresholds?

中文：运行日志里有记录。比如 9 月 18 日 00:10，Det09、Det10、Det11、Det15 的阈值被调高了。其他几次我还要去日志里查。

English: It is in the elog. For example, on 18 September at 00:10 the thresholds of Det09, Det10, Det11 and Det15 were raised. I still need to look up the other changes.

Q4. 模板具体怎么做的？/ How exactly are the templates made?

中文：每个脉冲：中值滤波、100 千赫低通、减基线、除以峰值，用两个指数拟合找起点，把实测脉冲按起点对齐，最后把所有 series 的脉冲取平均。拟合只用来找起点。

English: For each pulse: median filter, 100 kilohertz low-pass, subtract the baseline, divide by the peak, fit two exponentials to find the start, and shift the measured pulse to that start. Then we average the pulses of all series. The fit is only used to find the start.

Q5. 有些模板开头有尖峰？/ Some templates have a spike at the start?

中文：那是自己 LED 的格子，LED 驱动串进了读出。隔壁探测器看同一个 LED 是干净的，可以用那个模板。

English: Those are own-LED cells: the LED drive is picked up by the readout. The neighbouring detector sees the same LED cleanly, so we can use its template.

Q6. 例子脉冲为什么用 40 kHz 低通？/ Why the 40 kHz low-pass on the example pulses?

中文：为了去掉高频噪声，让 45 条线更容易比较。脉冲的上升和下降比这慢得多，所以形状基本不变。

English: To remove high-frequency noise, so the 45 pulses are easier to compare. The pulse rise and fall are much slower than that, so the shape hardly changes.
